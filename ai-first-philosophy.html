CELSIOR AGENT FRAMEWORK FOR ENTERPRISES (CAFÉ)

Celsior Agent Framework for Enterprises (CAFÉ)

MANAGED AI AGENTS-AS-A-SERVICE (PACE)

Managed AI Agents-as-a-Service (PACE)

PACE is the runtime and governance layer that connects agent estates across Agentforce, Azure AI, and Amazon Bedrock through a single unified fabric. It provides real-time observability, unified policy enforcement, drift detection, and SLA ownership across every platform simultaneously.

HUMAN-AI LOOP ORCHESTRATION (HALO)

Human-AI Loop Orchestration (HALO)

HALO is the layer that keeps qualified professionals in the accountability chain at every decision point that carries weight. Configurable review gates, escalation workflows, and full accountability audit trails ensure AI operates with speed while humans retain final authority.

Accountable by Architecture

Governance in enterprise AI is not a policy document or a post-deployment audit. It is a structural design choice made at the architecture stage, before a single agent goes into production.

Celsior's HALO framework operationalizes human oversight across every AI workflow. Review gates are configurable by output type, risk category, and business function. When an agent's output crosses a defined threshold of consequence, whether financial, legal, or operational, it routes to a qualified human reviewer before execution. That decision, along with its context and the reviewer's authority level, is logged into a full accountability audit trail.

For organizations preparing for the EU AI Act's mandatory human oversight requirements for high-risk systems, which take effect in August 2026, HALO provides a production-ready compliance path. Policy enforcement is encoded directly into the delivery pipeline, not applied retrospectively once a deployment is already under regulatory scrutiny.
