ROI varies by estate complexity and debt severity, but our completed engagements consistently show 40–60% reduction in ongoing maintenance costs and a 30–50% improvement in feature delivery velocity within 12 months. We quantify the expected ROI during the initial assessment phase before any commitment is made.

We use a strangler fig pattern combined with feature flags and parallel running environments to ensure zero-downtime migration. Every remediation effort begins with comprehensive regression test coverage before any changes are deployed to production.

Assessment typically takes 2–4 weeks. First measurable outcomes are usually visible within 90 days of remediation start. Full transformation engagements typically run 6–18 months depending on estate complexity.

We embed architectural governance, automated quality gates, and developer education as standard deliverables — not afterthoughts. Our Continuous Modernisation Factory model provides ongoing oversight to prevent regression.

Rewrites are high-risk, high-cost, and frequently fail. Our approach incrementally modernises existing systems, preserving business logic while replacing outdated infrastructure — reducing risk by an order of magnitude.

We use a risk-impact matrix combining business criticality, remediation cost, and downstream dependency analysis. High-impact, lower-risk items are tackled first to demonstrate ROI quickly and build organisational confidence.

Every engagement includes embedded pairing, living documentation, architecture decision records, and structured handoff ceremonies. We measure knowledge retention, not just delivery velocity.
